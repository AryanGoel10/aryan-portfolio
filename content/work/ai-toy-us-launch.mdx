## Context

180 Degrees Consulting NSUT, where I am now President, took on an AI smart-toy startup that wanted to launch in the US. I was **Project Lead**, running a **30-member team** from January to February 2026.

## The problem

A new product in a new country raises three questions at once: **who will buy it, what should it cost, and where should it launch first?** Getting any of them wrong at launch is expensive.

## What I did

1. **Market research** on the US opportunity and the customers most likely to buy.
2. **Pricing** for the US market.
3. **A state-wise rollout plan**, so the launch starts where it is most likely to work and expands from there.
4. **Led the team**: 30 people working towards one plan, delivered to the client.

<Callout title="Impact">
- **1,000+ pre-orders in the first week**, as reported by the client

*How to read this:* our team built the plan, and the client ran the launch and reported this result.
</Callout>
