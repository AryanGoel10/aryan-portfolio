## Context

I joined Nextvant in June 2025 as a Founder's Office Intern and became a Founder's Office Associate in December 2025. Until April 2026, I led go-to-market work for **three client companies, based in Australia, Europe and China**.

## The problem

Three clients, three different markets. Each needed more of the right people to find them, and more of those people to take action. That meant working on the whole journey, not just one channel.

## What I did

1. **SEO**, so each client's pages ranked for the searches their customers actually make.
2. **Google Ads**, to put the clients in front of people already searching for what they sell.
3. **Full-funnel campaigns** that took people from first seeing the brand through to converting, so the extra traffic turned into results.
4. **An India market-entry plan** for the China-based client, built around B2B outreach on LinkedIn.

<Callout title="Impact">
In 3 months:

- **+280% impressions:** the clients were seen nearly four times as often
- **+400% clicks:** five times as many people visited
- **+15% conversions:** more visitors took the action that mattered

*How to read this:* impressions → clicks → conversions is the journey from being seen, to being visited, to winning a customer.
</Callout>

## What I'd do next

Focus on conversion. Clicks grew much faster than conversions, so landing pages and offers are where the next gain is.
