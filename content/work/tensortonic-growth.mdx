## Context

TensorTonic is an ML coding-practice platform with 52K+ learners. I joined as GTM & Growth Engineer Intern in September 2026 and own growth end to end: paid ads, SEO/AEO, partnerships, content and retention.

## The problem

95% of search clicks were branded. People found TensorTonic only if they already knew the name. There was no paid acquisition channel at all, so every new learner depended on word of mouth.

## What I did

1. **Launched the first paid channel.** Five Google Ads search campaigns across the US and India, aimed at competitor and interview-prep intent. They ran at a 12.2% click-through rate.
2. **Fixed pages that were seen but not clicked.** I rewrote titles and meta descriptions on high-impression pages.
3. **Built non-branded and AI visibility.** I published comparison guides and got TensorTonic listed on external platforms, so it shows up in searches and AI answers that don't name it.
4. **Closed a partnership.** A developer contest with Gradium AI, with a 2M-credit prize pool, to drive engagement.
5. **Worked on retention.** I ran a win-back program for ~400 paying subscribers.
6. **Opened new channels.** B2B outbound with Apollo, and Instagram reels.

<Callout title="Impact">
- **+44% organic search clicks in 2 months** (Google Search Console, 15 Sep–12 Nov vs the prior 59 days)
- **Search CTR doubled, 1.7% → 3.6%**
- **93 sign-ups in 3 weeks** from Google Ads, at a 12.2% CTR
- **~60% of tracked ChatGPT and Google AI answers** now include TensorTonic
</Callout>
