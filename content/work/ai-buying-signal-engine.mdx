## Context

Nextvant sells GTM services. I rejoined in August 2026 in an expanded founder's office role, and part of that is building the engine that finds Nextvant's own next clients.

## The problem

Finding the right accounts by hand doesn't scale, and generic outreach gets ignored. I wanted a system that spots buying signals, qualifies the account and reaches out with a message that's actually about them.

## What I did

I built a pipeline of AI agents. Each step hands off to the next:

<Workflow />

1. **Scrape signals.** Apify collects buying signals from the web.
2. **Enrich and qualify.** Clay enriches each account and scores it; Apollo fills in contact details.
3. **Route to Slack.** Qualified accounts land in Slack.
4. **Write the message.** OpenAI/Claude APIs generate a personalised message for each account.
5. **Send.** Instantly handles email and HeyReach handles LinkedIn.

This is one of several workflows. I also build automations in n8n and Make, and intent-data workflows in HubSpot.

<Callout title="Impact">
- **3,000+ accounts scanned** for buying signals
- **20 meetings booked so far** (November 2026)
</Callout>
