## Context

From May to July 2026 I was an Associate Consultant Intern in Mastercard Advisors' Performance Analytics practice in Mumbai, working for India's #1 card issuer. Every quarter, the team produces quarterly business reviews (QBRs) for this client: files that show how its card business performed and where to act next.

*The client stays anonymous, and there are no screenshots or client data on this page, by design.*

## The problem

QBR production was manual. Each file took about **120 minutes** of pivot-table work, there were **10 file types**, and the total came to **20+ hours of analyst time every quarter**.

Manual work is also where mistakes creep in, in numbers a client relies on. And because it was manual, taking the same reports to more clients would have meant more hours, not fewer.

## What I did

1. **Built a parameter-driven pipeline** in Python and VBScript. You choose the inputs, run it, and get the finished file, with no copy-pasting or hand-built pivots.
2. **Made the metrics modular.** SME and market metrics are separate building blocks, so adding or changing a metric doesn't mean rewriting the pipeline.
3. **Made the output deterministic and auditable.** The same inputs always produce the same file, and every number can be traced back to its source.
4. **Designed it to scale** beyond this client, as a reporting framework the team can reuse for other banking clients.
5. **Handed it over to the team**, who adopted it as their standard way of producing QBRs.

<Callout title="Impact">
- **120 → 5 minutes per file, 95% faster**, across all 10 file types
- **20–25 analyst-hours saved every quarter**
- **Zero errors**, with output that can be audited line by line
- **Adopted as the team's standard workflow**

I left the internship with a full-time offer, joining in May 2027.
</Callout>
