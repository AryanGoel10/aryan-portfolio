## Context

TensorTonic is an ML coding-practice platform with 52K+ learners. I joined in September 2026 as GTM & Growth Engineer Intern and own growth end to end: paid ads, SEO/AEO, partnerships, content and retention.

## The problem

**95% of search clicks were branded.** People found TensorTonic only when they already knew its name, so search wasn't bringing in new learners. On top of that, there was **no paid acquisition channel** at all. Growth depended on word of mouth.

To grow, TensorTonic had to show up for people who had never heard of it, both in Google and in the AI tools people now ask for recommendations.

## What I did

### 1. Built a paid channel from zero

I launched five Google Ads search campaigns across the US and India, aimed at two kinds of intent: people comparing TensorTonic's competitors, and people preparing for ML interviews. The ads ran at a **12.2% click-through rate** (roughly 1 in 8 people who saw an ad clicked it) and brought in **93 sign-ups in the first 3 weeks**.

### 2. Fixed pages that were seen but not clicked

Some pages appeared often in Google but few people clicked them. I rewrote their titles and meta descriptions, the text people see in search results, so they matched what searchers were looking for.

### 3. Showed up where people search without the brand name

I published comparison guides and got TensorTonic listed on external platforms. This helps it appear for non-branded searches, and in the answers ChatGPT and Google AI give when someone asks for a tool like it.

### 4. Closed a partnership

I closed a developer contest with Gradium AI, with a 2M-credit prize pool, to drive engagement. It runs from 20 November to 20 December 2026.

### 5. Looked after existing customers

I ran a win-back program for about 400 paying subscribers.

### 6. Opened new channels

I started B2B outbound with Apollo, and Instagram reels.

<Callout title="Impact">
- **+44% organic search clicks in 2 months.** Source: Google Search Console, 15 September–12 November compared with the 59 days before.
- **Search CTR doubled, from 1.7% to 3.6%**, so the same Google visibility now brings in about twice the visits.
- **93 sign-ups in 3 weeks** from Google Ads, at a 12.2% CTR, from a channel that didn't exist before.
- **~60% of tracked ChatGPT and Google AI answers** now include TensorTonic.
</Callout>
