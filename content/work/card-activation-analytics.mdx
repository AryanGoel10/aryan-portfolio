## Context

On the same Mastercard Advisors internship, I worked on card activation for India's #1 card issuer. The question was simple: **which cardholders should the bank try to activate, and is it worth the money?**

*The client stays anonymous, and no client data appears on this page.*

## The problem

The bank had **5.2M credit cardholders**, and **2.6M debit cardholders who only used their cards at ATMs**, never to pay. An unused card earns the bank nothing.

Activation campaigns cost money, though. Offers, rewards and communication all add up, so targeting everyone wastes budget on people who won't respond, or who don't earn enough to pay back the offer.

## What I did

1. **Segmented the credit portfolio.** I grouped cardholders by activity state (how they use the card today) and card tier into **7 segments**.
2. **Chose where to focus.** I proposed an activation set of **5 of those segments**, the ones worth a campaign.
3. **Rebuilt the ROI model.** I modelled what each segment earns the bank through revolve (interest on balances) and interchange (fees on each payment), and turned it into a **Go/No-Go framework** the client can apply segment by segment.
4. **Ranked the debit cardholders.** For the 2.6M ATM-only debit cardholders, I built propensity tiers: a **High tier of ~280K** and a **Medium tier of ~2.25M** people, by how likely they are to start paying with their card.
5. **Checked the offers.** I modelled merchant-level offer economics, so each offer is judged on what it returns, not just how attractive it looks.

Along the way I analysed customer lifecycles, transactions and past campaign performance. Everything ran on **SQL and PySpark over Hadoop/Impala**.

<Callout title="Impact">
- **~₹500 Cr incremental present value** from the Go/No-Go framework on the credit portfolio
- **+12.59% projected activation uplift** for debit cardholders, against a baseline forecast of −0.72%
- **~140K projected new activations**

*How to read this:* these are projections from the models I built, not results after a campaign. The baseline forecast expected debit activation to fall slightly. The plan projects a rise instead.
</Callout>
