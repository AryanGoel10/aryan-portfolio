## Context

Through 180 Degrees Consulting NSUT, I worked as a Junior Consultant on a growth strategy for an EV company, from May to August 2025.

## The problem

The company had several directions it could grow in. It needed to know which ones to pursue and how.

## What I did

1. **Market analysis** across three verticals: OEM, ride-hailing and battery recycling.
2. **Competitive benchmarking** to see where the company stood against others in each vertical.
3. **Partnership options** that could open each vertical faster than going alone.

<Callout title="Impact">
- **40% sales increase**, as reported by the client
</Callout>

## What I'd do next

Check which of the three verticals drove the sales increase, and focus the next round of strategy there.
