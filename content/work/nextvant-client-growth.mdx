## Context

In my first stint at Nextvant (June 2025 – April 2026) I led GTM for three international clients, based in Australia, Europe and China.

## The problem

Three clients, three different markets. Each one needed more people to find them and more of those people to convert.

## What I did

1. **SEO** to grow organic visibility.
2. **Google Ads** to buy targeted traffic.
3. **Full-funnel campaigns** that took people from first impression to conversion.
4. **An India market-entry plan** for the China-based client, built on B2B LinkedIn outreach.

<Callout title="Impact">
Across the three clients, in 3 months:

- **+280% impressions**
- **+400% clicks**
- **+15% conversions**
</Callout>

<Tools items={["SEO", "Google Ads", "LinkedIn", "Go-to-Market Strategy"]} />

## What I'd do next

Push harder on conversion: clicks grew much faster than conversions, so the landing pages and offers are where the next gain is.
