## Context

Nextvant is a GTM and HubSpot services company that works with B2B businesses. I first worked there from June 2025 to April 2026, and rejoined in August 2026, after my Mastercard internship, in an expanded founder's office role. Alongside owning two client accounts, I lead the build of the system that finds Nextvant's own next clients.

## The problem

A services business lives on conversations with the right companies at the right time. Doing that by hand is slow: you have to research each company, decide whether it's a fit, find the right person and write a message that doesn't read like a template. Generic messages get ignored, and careful ones don't scale.

The goal was a system that does the research and first draft automatically, so outreach can be both personal and frequent.

## How the engine works

Each step hands its output to the next one:

<Workflow />

1. **Spot the signal.** Apify collects buying signals: public signs that a company may need GTM help now.
2. **Check the fit.** Clay enriches each account with company and contact details and scores it, and Apollo fills in contact information. Accounts that don't fit stop here.
3. **Route it.** Qualified accounts are posted to Slack, so the team sees every lead the engine finds.
4. **Write the first message.** OpenAI and Claude models draft a message specific to that company and its signal, not a mail-merge template.
5. **Send it.** Instantly sends the email sequence and HeyReach handles LinkedIn outreach.

## What I did

- **Designed the flow** from signal to sent message, deciding which tool does which job.
- **Built the agents and the connections** between Apify, Clay, Apollo, Slack, the AI models, Instantly and HeyReach.
- **Kept it running alongside client work**, as part of my founder's office role.

This is one of several automations I've built at Nextvant. I also build workflows in n8n and Make, and intent-data workflows in HubSpot.
