## Context

180 Degrees Consulting NSUT took on an AI smart-toy startup that wanted to launch in the US. I was Project Lead, running a 30-member team from January to February 2026.

## The problem

A new product in a new country: the client needed to know what to charge, where to launch first and who would buy.

## What I did

1. **Market research** on the US opportunity and the customers most likely to buy.
2. **Pricing** for the US market.
3. **A state-wise rollout plan**, so the launch starts where it's most likely to work.
4. **Led the team**: 30 people, one plan, delivered to the client.

<Callout title="Impact">
- **1,000+ pre-orders in week one**, as reported by the client
</Callout>
