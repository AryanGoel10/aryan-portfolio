## Context

Through 180 Degrees Consulting NSUT, I worked as a **Junior Consultant** on a growth strategy for an electric-vehicle company, from May to August 2025.

## The problem

The company could grow in several directions: selling to vehicle makers, working with ride-hailing fleets, or battery recycling. Pursuing all of them at once spreads a team thin. It needed to know where the opportunity was and how to go after it.

## What I did

1. **Market analysis** across the three verticals: OEM, ride-hailing and battery recycling.
2. **Competitive benchmarking**, to see where the company stood against competitors in each vertical.
3. **Partnership options** that could open each vertical faster than going alone.

<Callout title="Impact">
- **40% sales increase**, as reported by the client

*How to read this:* the client reported this figure after the engagement. Many things affect sales, and our strategy was one input.
</Callout>

Find out which of the three verticals drove the sales increase, and focus the next round of strategy there.
